Tóm tắt tiếng Việt: Word Chain không thu thập, không chia sẻ và không truyền dữ liệu cá nhân của người dùng. Dữ liệu cài đặt và thành tích chỉ được lưu cục bộ trên thiết bị.

1. Information We Collect

Word Chain does not collect personal information from users. The app does not require an account, login, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or payment information.

2. Local App Data

The app stores limited gameplay information locally on your device so the game can remember your preferences and progress. This may include:

  • App settings such as language, theme, and answer time.
  • Gameplay statistics such as scores, best chains, and achievements.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer or to any server by Word Chain.

3. Data Sharing

Word Chain does not sell, rent, share, or transfer user data to third parties. The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

4. Internet and Permissions

The release version of Word Chain is designed to work without a user account and without special Android permissions. The app does not use Internet access to upload personal data.

5. Children

Word Chain is a simple word game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Deletion

Since gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it by clearing the app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

7. Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we will use the information you provide only to respond to your request. Support emails are not used for advertising or tracking.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at quangluu1196@gmail.com.
